Text Message Consent

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Pawnshop Pal is the mobile app that Alpha Pawn Systems LLC, doing business as Apex Pawn, provides for customers of pawn shops that use Our point-of-sale software. Pawnshop Pal sends two kinds of text messages from (877) 385-7296: invites and sign-in codes. This page describes how people agree to receive each one. We write every message, and pawn shops cannot write or edit them. We do not send marketing text messages.

Invites: Verbal Consent at the Pawn Shop

An invite is a single text message with a link to download Pawnshop Pal. A customer receives one only after agreeing to it in person, at the counter of a pawn shop that uses Apex Pawn:

  1. A store employee reads the customer the script below and asks whether they would like the text.
  2. If the customer agrees, the employee reads the customer's mobile number back to them to confirm it.
  3. The employee checks "Customer agreed to receive this text" in Apex Pawn and presses Send. The box starts unchecked, and Send is unavailable until it is checked.

Only employees whom their store has given permission to send invites in Apex Pawn can send one. Apex Pawn records each invite with the employee who sent it, the date and time, and the phone number it was sent to.

Agreeing is optional. Declining does not affect the customer's business with the pawn shop: their loans, buys, sales, or anything else.

Script

Employee: Would you like a one-time text from Pawnshop Pal with a link to download the app? It shows your account with us. It's optional, and saying no doesn't change anything about your loans, buys, sales, or anything else with us. Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Customer: Yes.

Employee: Is your mobile number 555-123-4567?

Customer: Yes.

The employee checks "Customer agreed to receive this text" and presses Send.

The Invite Message

Every invite is the same text:

Pawnshop Pal: Your pawnshop invited you to its customer app. Get it at https://pal.apexpawn.com/get/
Reply STOP to opt out.

An invite expires after 7 days. We send no reminders or follow-up messages.

Sign-In Codes: Requested in the App

A person who chooses to sign in to Pawnshop Pal with a phone number enters the number in the app and taps Send Code. The screen tells them, before they tap, that We will text them a code. Each request sends one message:

123456 is your Pawnshop Pal sign-in code. Do not share it with anyone.

People who do not want to receive texts can sign in with an email address instead.
